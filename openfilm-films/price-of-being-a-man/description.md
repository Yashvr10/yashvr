# The Price Of Being A Man

**Title:** The Price Of Being A Man

**Description:**

A boy walks into a dark forest, and some time later, a man walks out. Nobody is born strong; strength is hammered into shape, and the heat that does the shaping is rarely gentle. This is a slow, reflective talk about what it costs to become a man: accepting that no one is coming to save you, carrying weight on purpose, keeping your fire, putting your life in order, living by a code, the burden of purpose, and the second birth that turns a boy into a man.
