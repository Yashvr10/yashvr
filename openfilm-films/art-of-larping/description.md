# The Art of LARPING: How to Act Your Ideal Self Into Existence

**Title:** The Art of LARPING: How to Act Your Ideal Self Into Existence

**Description:**

One sleepless night at 4 AM, I sketched out the man I wanted to be, and when the sun rose I simply began acting as if. This is not the larping of rented Lambos and fake lives. It's the oldest method there is for becoming who you really are: Alexander sleeping with the Iliad under his pillow, the wax masks of Rome's ancestors, Aristotle's "we become builders by building", the Hermetic art of imagining yourself into anything. Build your own myth, decide you already are it, and play the character until the mask fuses to the skin.

"We are what we pretend to be, so we must be careful about what we pretend to be." Kurt Vonnegut
