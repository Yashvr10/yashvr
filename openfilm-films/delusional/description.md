# Delusional Until It Becomes Real

**Title:** Delusional Until It Becomes Real | The Kind of Self-Belief That Looks Like Madness

**Description:**

They will call you delusional long before they call you successful. This is about that kind of belief: holding on to a picture of your life that nobody else can see yet, through the stares, the doubt and the people who love you telling you to quit.

Delusion is simply faith that hasn't clocked in yet.

"All men dream, but not equally. The dreamers of the day are dangerous men, for they may act their dream with open eyes, to make it possible." T. E. Lawrence

0:30 I. The stares
2:34 II. Faith that hasn't clocked in
5:25 III. Refuse to wake up
