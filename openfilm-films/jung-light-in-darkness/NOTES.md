# Carl Jung: The Light in the Darkness: notes

- Requested: a 20-minute film on the same topic as "Carl Jung: A Journey into the Depths of the Soul" (Eternalised),
  with similar visuals. The user supplied that video's transcript.
- That transcript is almost entirely Jung's own text from *Memories, Dreams, Reflections* (1962) plus late interviews,
  which are still in copyright. So the narration here is an ORIGINAL script that retells the same life in our own words,
  following the same arc, with only a handful of short, attributed quotations.
- Visuals: Eternalised leans on Jung's *Red Book* paintings, which are copyrighted (Foundation of the Works of C.G. Jung).
  We use public-domain look-alikes instead: alchemical engravings (Splendor Solis, Rosarium, Maier, Mutus Liber),
  Symbolist and Romantic paintings (Redon, Böcklin, Friedrich, Blake, Doré), mandalas and Tibetan thangkas from museum
  open-access collections. Photos of Jung himself are not used.
- Voice: the user's own "My Voice".
