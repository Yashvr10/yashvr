# Carl Jung: The Light in the Darkness

**Title:** Carl Jung: The Light in the Darkness | His Journey Into the Unconscious

**Description:**

Carl Jung said his life was the story of what rose up from inside him. A child's nightmare of a one-eyed god beneath the earth. A stone in the garden he could not tell apart from himself. A dream of a tiny flame carried through fog, with a giant shadow behind it. A vision of Europe drowning in blood, months before the First World War. Then the descent: the Red Book, Philemon, the mandalas, the alchemists, the stone tower at Bollingen, and a vision at the edge of death, floating high above the earth.

This is the inner story of Carl Jung, told in his spirit and with a few of his own words, and what it asks of us: to kindle a light in the darkness of mere being.

"The sole purpose of human existence is to kindle a light in the darkness of mere being." Carl Jung
