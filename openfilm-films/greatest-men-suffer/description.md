## Title
Why the Greatest Men Must Suffer

## Description
Nietzsche claimed that great suffering has produced "all the elevations of humanity hitherto". Was he right? A deaf composer, a one-handed prisoner, a blind poet, a slave and an emperor: the men history remembers did not suffer less than us. They suffered, and built anyway.
