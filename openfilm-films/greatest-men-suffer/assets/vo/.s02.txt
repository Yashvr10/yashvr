bm_george|0.82|In eighteen eighty-six, Friedrich Nietzsche asked a question that still unsettles us. The discipline of suffering, of great suffering, he wrote, know ye not that it is only this discipline that has produced all the elevations of humanity hitherto? The tension of soul in misfortune, he went on, which communicates to it its energy. Its inventiveness and bravery in undergoing, enduring, interpreting, and exploiting misfortune.
