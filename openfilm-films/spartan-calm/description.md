# The Spartan Secret to Staying Calm Under Pressure

**Title:** The Spartan Secret to Staying Calm Under Pressure

**Description:**

At seven years old, a Spartan boy was handed to the agoge: one cloak a year, no shoes, barely enough food, thirteen years of training. Not to make war easier, but to make it ordinary.

At Thermopylae, told the Persian arrows would blot out the sun, Dienekes answered: "Good. Then we will fight in the shade." This is the Spartan secret to staying calm under pressure: you don't summon calm in the moment. You build it, by making the challenge familiar long before it arrives.
