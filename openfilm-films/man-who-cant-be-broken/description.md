# YouTube copy: The Man Who Can't Be Broken

## Title
The Man Who Can't Be Broken

## Description
A Roman holding a bridge under a rain of spears. A 31-year-old lawyer who called himself "the most miserable man living". A bankrupt writer locked in the pillory. Odysseus telling his own heart to endure. The man who can't be broken isn't the man who can't be hurt. He's the man who doesn't let a bad chapter write the rest of the book. This film is about the spears every man takes, and what he does next.
