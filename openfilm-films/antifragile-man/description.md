# The Antifragile Man

**Title:** The Antifragile Man

**Description:**

There's a kind of man who'll never make it. Two years ago, I was him: a boring life, the same routine every day, no adventures and no stories, all because I was scared. This is how I changed: from the pessimist who says no to everything and plans every detail, to the optimist who says yes, leaves room for the unknown and turns every bad thing into fuel. Plus the night we climbed a snowy couloir in the Swiss Alps in the dark, and why it was the most alive I have ever felt.

"One must still have chaos in oneself to be able to give birth to a dancing star." Friedrich Nietzsche
