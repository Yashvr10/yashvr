# YouTube copy: A Man Needs a Why

## Title
A Man Needs a Why

## Description
"He who has a why to live for can bear almost any how." An emperor who didn't want to get out of bed, a Roman who said life is not short but wasted, a man at Walden Pond who saw quiet desperation in his comfortable neighbours, and a 2019 study of almost 7,000 adults. This film asks what a purpose is, what happens to a man without one, and how to build one. You don't find a purpose waiting for you. You build it by doing the work.
