# Live For Yourself, Not For Others

**Title:** Live For Yourself, Not For Others

**Description:**

"Every man loves himself more than all the rest of men, and yet sets less value on his own opinion of himself than on the opinion of others." Marcus Aurelius

Even an emperor could not stop wondering what other people thought of him. This film follows the psychologist Alfred Adler and the ideas made famous by The Courage to Be Disliked (Ichiro Kishimi and Fumitake Koga): why the need for approval is a trap, how to separate your tasks from other people's, and why the freest people in history (the Impressionists laughed out of the Paris Salon, Diogenes in the street, Socrates before his jury) were willing to be disliked.
