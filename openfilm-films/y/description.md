# Life Won't Wait For You, So Fly

**Title:** Life Won't Wait For You, So Fly

**Description:**

Are you on the right path? Backing yourself when the world is against you can break walls, or break you. This is a slow, calm reflection on choosing the right wall to climb: picturing the life you want before you chase it, seeing past what your environment taught you to want, listening to your intuition, and making a little progress every day.

Don't put a fulfilling life on a pedestal. Get onto its level, and stay there. So let it.

Narration voice: synthetic (Kokoro).
