# How to Make Your Dream a Reality

**Title:** How to Make Your Dream a Reality

**Description:**

"If you have built castles in the air, your work need not be lost; that is where they should be. Now put the foundations under them." Henry David Thoreau

In 1903, the head of the Smithsonian had fifty thousand dollars, a staff of engineers and the whole press watching. Two bicycle mechanics from Ohio had their own shop and about a thousand dollars. Only one of them flew. This is the story of how a dream leaves your head and enters the world: the two traps that keep it a castle in the air, and the five foundations that put the ground beneath it.
