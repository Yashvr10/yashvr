# The Battle of Thoughts: Spiritual Warfare in Everyday Life

**Title:** The Battle of Thoughts: Spiritual Warfare in Everyday Life

**Description:**

You wake up tired, reach for your phone, and the day's small battles begin: envy, pride, anger, lust, sloth. And by night you promise that tomorrow will be different. This is a slow, reflective talk about the war that is fought in our thoughts: the mind as an inner fortress, the desert monks' practice of talking back to temptation, the Stoic idea of first movements, the quiet ways the devil works through deception, division, diversion and discouragement, and the healing of forgiveness, repentance and grace.

"The mind is its own place, and in itself can make a heaven of hell, a hell of heaven." John Milton, Paradise Lost
