# You Were Programmed: How Your Subconscious Was Written, and How to Rewrite It

**Title:** You Were Programmed: How Your Subconscious Was Written, and How to Rewrite It

**Description:**

There is a version of you that flinches before anyone says anything critical, sabotages the good thing right when it starts working, and calls it "just who I am." You didn't choose it. It was written into you before you could argue with it. This is not manifestation or vibes: it's the real mechanics of how a belief gets installed in your nervous system, and how it gets uninstalled. Hebbian learning, neuroplasticity, implicit memory, the gap between stimulus and reaction, identity, emotion, and evidence over affirmation.
