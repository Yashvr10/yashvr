# Metacognition: Catch Your Next Thought

**Title:** Metacognition: Catch Your Next Thought

**Description:**

Try to catch your next thought. You can't, and in that moment there were two of you: one thinking, and one waiting. That is metacognition. It isn't your thoughts that create your reality, but your relationship with them. From "know thyself" at Delphi, through Socrates and Epictetus, to John Flavell's experiments at Stanford: how a thought becomes a belief, where the machinery breaks, and how to catch, judge and recalibrate your thoughts.
