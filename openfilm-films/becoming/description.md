# Becoming What You Are Meant To Become

**Title:** Becoming What You Are Meant To Become

**Description:**

The acorn does not try to become an oak. It only stops pretending to be anything else. This is a slow, reflective talk about the oldest advice there is, "Become what you are": noticing the fences other people built in you, wearing your masks lightly, not forcing the flower, the pain of transformation, the way of water, and the courage to live a life true to yourself before it is too late.

"Become what you are." Pindar
