# Why Believing in Yourself Feels So Lonely

**Title:** Why Believing in Yourself Feels So Lonely | Carl Jung on the Mask, the Shadow and Coming Home

**Description:**

Most people think believing in yourself looks like confidence: a straighter back, a louder voice. It doesn't. Real self-belief is quieter, and the first thing it costs you is the version of you that other people preferred.

Through Carl Jung's ideas of the persona (the mask), the shadow, and individuation, this film follows what happens when you stop abandoning yourself to keep the peace. It covers why people pull away when you change, the gold hidden in the parts of you that you were taught to hide, the price of always being nice, why walking alone is part of the road, the expectations we inherit, and what it means to finally come home to yourself.

"Who looks outside, dreams. Who looks inside, awakes." Carl Jung
