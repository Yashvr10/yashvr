## s070 (timeline) - chapter: The town they lived in
The modern answer starts in the sand. In nineteen eighty eight, archaeologists began digging a lost town south of the Sphinx. In nineteen ninety, a tourist's horse stumbled on the builders' tombs. Then came a logbook.

## s080 (image)
The town covered more than seven hectares. The crown built it, with bakeries, workshops and royal grain silos. It looks less like a slave camp than a supply base.

## s083 (stat)
At its centre stood a royal administrative building with ten massive grain silos. Grain came in, and bread and beer went out to the workforce. In Arabic, the site is called the Wall of the Crow.

## s105 (quote) - chapter: Names on the stones

## s107 (flow)
